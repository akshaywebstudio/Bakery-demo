Made in-house, nothing shipped in

A neighbourhood oven, not a factory line.

Sweet Crumbs started as a single countertop mixer and a Saturday market stall. A decade later, we're still doing things the slow way: long-fermented doughs, butter laminated by hand, and a menu short enough that every item gets real attention.

Everything you see in the case today was mixed, shaped, or baked within the last twelve hours. When we sell out, we sell out — we'd rather stop than pull something from a freezer.

  • Long fermentation, 18–36 hours depending on the dough
  • Butter and eggs from farms within 40 miles
  • Vegan and gluten-free options baked in a separate line
